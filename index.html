// WHO AM I

About Me

My dream was to work with nature—to study plants, insects, and the environment. Life brought me to IT instead, but I still carry that creative spark through arts, drawing, recycling, and crafting terrariums.


I am upfront about my coding journey: I rely heavily on AI tools to build my programs as I learn the basics step by step.
